How It Works

From your first enquiry to your follow-up, here is what to expect.

Your journey, step by step

  1. Send an enquiry

    Use the contact form or WhatsApp to tell us a little about what you would like and your preferred way to be contacted.

  2. Confirm your booking

    We reply with available times, the price and how to pay.

  3. Attend your consultation

    Meet the reader online or in person at the agreed time. We will confirm the details with you when you book.

  4. Follow-up

    If you have questions afterwards, you can get in touch.

Frequently asked questions

How does an online reading work?

Online readings are held remotely at a time we agree with you, so you can join from anywhere. We will confirm how the session will be held and how long it lasts when you book.

What do I need for an online reading?

A phone, tablet or computer with a stable internet connection, and a quiet place where you will not be interrupted.

How should I prepare?

Think about the questions you would like to explore. If the reader needs any details from you, we will tell you when you book.

How do I book an appointment?

Send an enquiry through the contact form or WhatsApp. We reply during business hours, 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM IST, with available times, the price and how to pay.

I live in another country. Can I still book?

Yes. Tell us your country and we will suggest times that suit your time zone.

What if I need to reschedule or cancel?

Please contact us as early as you can if you need to change your appointment, and we will agree a new time with you.

How do I pay?

We will share the accepted payment methods and when payment is due when you send your enquiry.

Can you guarantee what the reading says?

No. Readings are offered for guidance and reflection, and no outcome is promised or guaranteed.

Is my information kept private?

Your details are used only to respond to your enquiry and arrange your consultation. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Still have questions?

We are happy to answer anything before you book.
