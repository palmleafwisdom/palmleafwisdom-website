What is palm leaf reading?

Palm leaf reading is a traditional practice in which guidance is drawn from writings kept on dried palm leaves. Palm leaf reading is a traditional practice associated with ancient manuscripts written on palm leaves. The reading process and traditions may vary depending on the practitioner. Readings are offered for reflection and guidance, not as guaranteed predictions.

Begin Your Palm Leaf Journey

Discover how the traditional palm leaf reading process works, from your first enquiry to your online reading.

  1. Place Your Order

    Contact Palmleaf Wisdom through WhatsApp or our contact form. Our team will explain the service, pricing, payment arrangements and next steps.

  2. Submit Your Thumbprint

    Follow the instructions provided by our team to submit your thumbprint for the traditional palm leaf identification process.

  3. The Palm Leaf Search

    The Library Team uses your submitted information to guide the search for the relevant palm leaf manuscripts. Our team will update you about the next stage.

  4. Arrange Your Reading

    Once your palm leaf has been identified and an appointment can be arranged, our team will explain the available options and session details.

  5. Attend Your Online Reading

    Join your scheduled session remotely and listen as the reader shares the information and interpretations from your identified palm leaf.
