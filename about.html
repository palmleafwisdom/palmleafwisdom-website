Our background

Palm Leaf Wisdom offers traditional palm leaf readings, together with ceremonies such as homa and athma shanti pooja, and mantra and yantra services.

Our services are available in person and online, for visitors in India and around the world.

A tradition with deep roots

For many centuries, palm leaves were used across South Asia to record texts, records and teachings. Writing was etched onto prepared leaves, which were then bound together with cord. Palm leaf reading draws on this heritage.

Our readings are offered for reflection and guidance, with respect for the tradition they come from.
